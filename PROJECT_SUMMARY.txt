PROBLEM SOLVED:
---------------
A 250-V d.c. shunt motor, taking an armature current of 150 A and running at
550 r.p.m. is braked by reversing the connections to the armature and inserting
additional resistance in series with it.

ANSWERS:
--------
(a) Series Resistance Required:  1.9371 Ω  (≈ 1.94 Ω)
(b) Initial Braking Torque:       985.49 N·m
(c) Torque at 200 rpm:            680.62 N·m

                         TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

PHYSICS MODELS:
---------------
• Electrical: V + Eb = Ia·(Ra+Rs) + La·dIa/dt
• Mechanical: J·dω/dt = -(T_brake + T_friction)
• Thermal: Cth·dT/dt = P_total - T/Rth
• Temperature coupling: Ra(T) = Ra₀·(1 + α·ΔT)

NUMERICAL METHODS:
------------------
• RK45: Adaptive Runge-Kutta 4-5 with error control
• Euler: Fixed-step explicit method
• Event detection: Automatic stop when motor stops
• Stability: Automatic step size adjustment
