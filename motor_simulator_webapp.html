Example 29.42: DC Shunt Motor Efficiency Analysis

Given Parameters:

  • Rated Output Power: 7.46 kW
  • Supply Voltage: 250 V
  • No-load Line Current: 5 A
  • Armature Resistance: 0.5 Ω
  • Field Resistance: 250 Ω

Step 1: Field Current (Constant)

I_sh = V / R_sh = 250 / 250 = 1.0 A

Step 2: No-load Armature Current

I_a0 = I_L0 - I_sh = 5 - 1 = 4.0 A

Step 3: Constant Losses

P_input(no-load) = 250 × 5 = 1250 W

P_cu(field) = 250 × 1 = 250 W

P_cu(armature,no-load) = 4² × 0.5 = 8 W

P_constant = 1250 - 250 - 8 = 992 W

Step 4: Full Load Armature Current

Output Power = E_b × I_a = (V - I_a×R_a) × I_a

7460 = (250 - 0.5×I_a) × I_a

0.5×I_a² - 250×I_a + 7460 = 0

Using quadratic formula:

I_a = 31.87 A (practical value)

Step 5: Full Load Efficiency

E_b = 250 - 31.87 × 0.5 = 234.06 V

P_cu(armature) = 31.87² × 0.5 = 507.5 W

Total Losses = 992 + 250 + 507.5 = 1749.5 W

P_input = 7460 + 1749.5 = 9209.5 W

Efficiency = (7460 / 9209.5) × 100 = 81.0%

Step 6: Maximum Efficiency Condition

Max efficiency when: Variable Losses = Constant Losses

I_a² × R_a = P_constant + P_cu(field)

I_a² × 0.5 = 992 + 250 = 1242

I_a = 49.84 A

E_b = 250 - 49.84 × 0.5 = 225.08 V

P_output(max eff) = 225.08 × 49.84 = 11.22 kW

📊 Summary & Conclusions:

Efficiency at Full Load (7.46 kW): 81.0%
Maximum Efficiency: 83.3%
Output at Max Efficiency: 11.22 kW
Is Max Efficiency Achievable? ⚠️ NO - Requires 50% overload

⚠️ Important Note: The output power for maximum efficiency (11.22 kW) exceeds the rated output (7.46 kW) by 50%. Operating at this level would overload the machine and is NOT recommended for continuous operation due to:

  • Excessive heating beyond design limits
  • Accelerated insulation degradation
  • Reduced machine lifespan
  • Potential mechanical stress damage
